# Code of Conduct

This is a hobby project. Keep it a place people want to come back to.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient, including with people new to N64 internals, modding or Git.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith. Ask before assuming someone is wrong.
- Keep discussion on topic for the issue or pull request it's in.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discrimination based on age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience level, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual orientation.
- Posting other people's private information.
- Sharing, requesting or linking ROMs or other copyrighted game data.
- Spam, or repeatedly pushing a point after the maintainer has made a decision.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block anyone who breaks these rules, temporarily or permanently.

To report something, contact [the maintainer](https://github.com/MikeSemicolonD) privately or use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or user.

This applies in the repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere someone represents the project.

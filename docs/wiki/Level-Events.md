# Level reference

What each marker in a level does. Click the **?** on an item's card or panel to jump to its section. To change these, see [Make a level mod](Make-a-Level-Mod).

- [Starts and cameras](Level-Starts-and-Cameras): where the player, wingmen and cameras begin.
- [Paths](Level-Paths): the routes units fly, and the events along them.
- [Trigger areas](Level-Trigger-Areas): spots that make something happen when reached.
- [Regions](Level-Regions): areas that change music, terrain or ground units.

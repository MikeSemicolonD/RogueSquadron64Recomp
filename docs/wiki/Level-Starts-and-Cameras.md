# Starts and cameras

Part of the [Level reference](Level-Events).

## Player start / restarts

Where the player begins, and where they return after being shot down. The cone shows the facing.

## Wingmen

Where the wingmen start. Without a [path](Level-Paths), a wingman stays where it is placed.

## Other units / objects

Placed units or objects the viewer has no model for, such as trains, pilots and landing pads. The cone shows the facing.

## Cameras

Camera positions for intros, endings and camera shots. The pyramid opens toward where it looks. A new one is placed at your view.

## Menu points

Hangar camera starts and bridge scene points in the front end.

## Devastator gun settings

Settings for the World Devastator's gun turrets, all at one spot. The guns are part of the World Devastator model.

# Trigger areas

Part of the [Level reference](Level-Events).

## Goal areas

Fires when the player or a chosen unit comes within its **Radius**. It can complete a Reach an area objective, bring in a wave, play a camera shot or set off a rule.

## Wave areas

Like a goal area, and also brings in the unit it names.

## Voice-line areas

Flying in makes the mission play a voice line. The stock mission script picks which.

## Objective arrow points

The HUD arrow points at the nearest one whose objective allows it.

## Other triggers

Trigger areas the game never reads. Lines point at what they name.

## Lava vents

Spots that erupt lava (Raid on Sullust).

# Rogue Squadron 64 Recompiled wiki

These pages show you how to mod with the [RS64 Explorer](https://mikesemicolond.github.io/RogueSquadron64Recomp/tools/web/rs64-explorer/).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> These documents are still a **work in progress**.
> The explorer functionality works but UI/UX still needs work.

## Start here

- [Getting started](RS64-Explorer): what you need, moving around the view, and the shortcuts.
- [Saving, testing and sharing](Saving-Testing-and-Sharing): projects, undo, trying your mod in the game, and exporting it.

## Pick what you want to make

| Change an intro or ending cutscene, or an opening crawl | [Make a cutscene mod](Make-a-Cutscene-Mod) |
| Add, hide or move menu buttons | [Make a menu mod](Make-a-Menu-Mod) |
| Build a whole new mission with its own cutscenes, models and textures | [Make a custom level](Make-a-Custom-Level) |
